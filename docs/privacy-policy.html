Privacy Policy — Net Speed

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Net Speed does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. Everything the app measures stays on your device, and no user data is sent to us or to any third party.

What the app stores, and where

Net Speed measures network throughput and data usage and keeps the results in a database on your own device. Nothing is uploaded. Specifically, the app stores:

  • Daily totals of Wi-Fi and mobile data used
  • Your settings — data limit, billing cycle day, units, notification and overlay preferences

This data never leaves the device. Uninstalling Net Speed deletes all of it.

Special permissions

Two permissions cannot be requested from inside an app — you grant them yourself in system settings, and you can revoke them there at any time. Net Speed works without both; each one only enables the feature described below.

Usage access PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS

What it is used forWhat it is not used for
Reading Android's own network accounting so the app can show which apps used data, and so daily totals match the figures in Android Settings rather than being estimated from sampling. It is never used to see which apps you open, how long you use them, or when. The app reads network statistics only, and the results are displayed on your device and stored nowhere else.

Display over other apps SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW

What it is used forWhat it is not used for
Drawing the optional floating speed overlay — a small, draggable readout you can position anywhere on screen. It is never used to read, capture, or interact with anything shown by other apps. The overlay only draws text and cannot see what is beneath it.

Network connections

Net Speed makes no network requests for its normal operation. Speed and usage are measured from counters the Android system already maintains on the device.

There is one exception, and it only happens when you press the button:

  • The speed test. When you start a test, the app transfers a quantity of meaningless bytes to and from Cloudflare's public speed-test endpoint (speed.cloudflare.com) and times how long it takes. No information about you, your device, or your usage is included in those requests. As with any request to any server on the internet, your IP address is visible to that server — this is unavoidable for any speed test, and it is the only way the app ever contacts an outside service. The result is shown on screen and is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Latency measurement. While the floating overlay is visible, the app opens a brief connection to a public DNS server (8.8.8.8:53) to time the round trip. No data is sent or received beyond establishing and closing the connection.

App names and icons

The per-app usage breakdown shows the names and icons of apps installed on your device. These are read from the device's own package manager. No list of your installed apps is transmitted anywhere.

Children

Net Speed is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.
